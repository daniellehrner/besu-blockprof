Drag across the timeline to pick a time range. Click a thread or pool name on the left of the timeline to look at just that thread or pool.

The flame graph draws every call level, like async-profiler does. Tick “Fold nested and pass-through calls” for a shallower graph: a bar labelled “first › n frames › last” is then a run of calls that only pass through, and “×8” marks 8 nested calls of the same method; hover a bar for the full list.

“All blocks” adds the request up over every block in the capture, which gives more samples per method. The timeline and its time range stay with one block.

The search takes a regular expression and matches full frame names; click a method in the table to search for it. A removed stack only leaves the flame graph: the methods table and the RocksDB split still count it.
